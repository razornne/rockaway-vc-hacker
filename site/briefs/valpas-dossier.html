  • CorroboratedProduct built: smart anti‑bedbug bed legs, IoT platform, mobile/web apps and a dashboard that claim real‑time monitoring and in‑app image recognition.[1, 2, 14]
  • VerifiedPublic product surfaces and apps: app.valpashotels.com, dashboard and api subdomains plus iOS and Android app listings.[26, 27, 43]

  • Analyst viewEdge supported by public evidence. Granted patent EP3468360B1 evidences proprietary IP for the furniture‑leg concept; independent customer mention confirms at least some commercial deployment.[8, 7, 6]Assumes: Patent maps to the shipped hardware design
  • Analyst viewCannot be judged from outside. Image‑recognition accuracy, trap capture rate, long‑term elimination efficacy and false positive rates cannot be judged externally without raw model performance, telemetry and field logs.[9, 24, 14]Assumes: No public performance datasets provided

Risks and caveats

  • Analyst viewCustomer verification gap: multiple named case studies lack independent corroboration, raising concentration and revenue‑quality risk.[11, 12, 13]
  • Analyst viewClaims vs. evidence mismatch: strong commercialization and CO2/NOI benefit claims exist but documented, audited impact metrics are not provided.[40, 22]
  • Analyst viewDependency on third‑party LLMs for visibility product could create vendor risk if integrations or pricing change.[23, 37]

Questions for founders

  • Provide executed customer contracts or invoices for a sample of named hotels (e.g., RUNO, Kamisuwa, Sokos) to substantiate recurring revenue claims and install counts.[11, 44, 12]
  • Share field performance metrics: per‑room capture rate, image‑recognition precision/recall, and telemetry that supports the “permanent protection” claim.[9, 24]
  • Produce audited unit economics showing installation margin, recurring fees recognition (capitalised vs revenue) and payback period referenced to the pricing claim.[15, 38]
  • Clarify patent scope and freedom‑to‑operate: provide the legal claim set mapping to shipped hardware and any third‑party licence dependencies.[8, 10]
