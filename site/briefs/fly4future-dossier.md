- **Against:** Net result fell to 8\.8M CZK from 16\.6M while personnel costs rose to 30\.4M from 19\.4M CZK, showing spend growth ahead of profit\. *(Analyst view)* ^[4](https://or.justice.cz/ias/ui/vypis-sl-detail?dokument=92247881&subjektId=995597&spis=1101858)
