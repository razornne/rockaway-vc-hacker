Risks and caveats

  • Analyst viewUnverified security/compliance: SOC 2 and deployment-mode claims cannot be confirmed from public trust portals; SOC 2 report must be requested.[11, 39, 12]
  • Analyst viewDependency/risk on external LLM integrations for value and cost routing (OpenAI, Anthropic) could create vendor or pricing exposure.[21, 36]

Questions for founders

  • Please provide the SOC 2 Type II report and auditor name to confirm security and deployment claims (prompted by the not_checkable verification).[11]
  • Provide 2 reference enterprise customers with private‑cloud deployments and a brief technical contact to confirm in‑memory transient analysis and zero retention behavior.[9, 10]
  • Detail how model routing and token‑pricing work operationally: which LLM APIs are used in‑product versus passed through customer accounts?[3, 36]
  • Share unit economics: conversion rate from trial to Pro ($30/dev/mo) and price list uptake for $4,500 AI ROI reports to validate revenue assumptions.[29, 41]
