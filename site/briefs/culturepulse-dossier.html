Technical due diligence

What is built, who owns it, what could be rebuilt, and what to verify.

Ownership and capital

  • CorroboratedReported financing: Seed 'just south of $1M' led by Zero Gravity (2023) and a 2025 round reported at $1.5M / €1.45M led/backed by Rockaway Ventures with B Ventures and Simpact noted.[4, 5, 30, 31]
  • CorroboratedCompany states it will use proceeds to expand headcount and accelerate product development and go‑to‑market.[29, 32]

Technology profile

  • CorroboratedProduct capabilities (company claims): multilingual text analysis, 93‑dimension psychological profiling, behavioral digital twins for testing messaging and risk prediction (ARES branding cited).[27, 12, 3]Assumes: These are company/press claims, not independent verifications.
  • VerifiedEngineering footprint: public GitHub org with multiple repos (research and tooling), several active pushes including a 2026 frontend repo; cloud usage indicated (Amazon S3).[33, 34, 10, 35]
  • VerifiedAI dependency: no supplied facts name third‑party LLM vendors as required subprocessors or integrations; evidence primarily shows academic papers and in‑house modeling claims.[8, 9, 36]

What could be rebuilt, and what cannot be judged from outside

  • Analyst viewReplicable with off-the-shelf parts. Linguistic feature extraction and 93‑dimension psychological profiling could be reproduced using open NLP libraries and standard psychometric scales if datasets are available (public repos show relevant research code).[10, 27]Assumes: Access to the same training/labelled datasets is required.
  • Analyst viewEdge supported by public evidence. Peer‑reviewed papers and published simulation code indicate academic credibility and domain expertise, supporting a research edge rather than a proven production moat.[8, 9, 10]Assumes: Papers represent affiliated research but not deployed model weights.
  • Analyst viewCannot be judged from outside. True production accuracy, multilingual generalization, data pipelines, and model robustness cannot be judged from public papers and repos alone without held‑out evaluations and code/models.[11, 34]Assumes: External test datasets and internal evaluations are unavailable publicly.

Risks and caveats

  • Analyst viewUnverified performance claim: the '≈95% clinical accuracy correlation' figure is asserted in press coverage but lacks independent verification in supplied facts.[11, 18]
  • Analyst viewConcentration risk in investor base for recent round: reported reliance on a small number of lead VCs for the 2025 round.[5, 30, 31]
  • Analyst viewCommercial validation gap: no named paying customers were found in the supplied facts despite an AWS Marketplace seller profile and repeated press coverage.[7, 6]

Questions for founders

  • Request: provide held‑out evaluation datasets, evaluation protocol and confusion matrices underpinning the '95% correlation' claim referenced in press.[11]
  • Request: list paying customers or signed purchase contracts and provide redacted references for enterprise pilots versus R&D collaborations.[7, 6]
  • Request: supply details on multilingual training—corpora, annotation methodology, and out‑of‑language evaluations supporting 80–200+ language claims.[12, 13]
  • Request: codebase maturity and deployment architecture evidence (CI, model serving, data retention/compliance) beyond public GitHub repos.[34, 10, 35]
