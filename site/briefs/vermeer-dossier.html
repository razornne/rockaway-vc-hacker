Summary

  • Partly verifiedStage & traction. Closed a $10M Series A with Draper Associates and others; press cites >30 customers and partnerships with defence organisations and drone OEMs.[4, 5, 6, 7]
  • CorroboratedTechnology & moat. Claims centre on passive, multi‑camera visual navigation using prebuilt maps and AI—harder to replicate at scale because of training data and mapping, though public evidence for proprietary IP is mixed.[8, 9, 10, 11]
  • Analyst viewKey risk. Material IP and technical-uniqueness claims are inconsistent with an independent patent check that did not find Vermeer publications under that assignee.[12, 13, 14, 15]
  • Analyst viewDiligence focus. Verify asserted patents/ownership, demand representative flight logs and mapping/data provenance, and obtain redacted customer purchase orders to confirm revenue and integration depth.[15, 12, 6]

Technical due diligence

What is built, who owns it, what could be rebuilt, and what to verify.

Ownership and capital

  • Partly verifiedSeries A: multiple sources report a closed $10M Series A (lead mentions include Draper Associates; named participants include Rockaway Ventures and US Air Force Techstars).[5, 4, 17, 18]
  • Company claimOperational footprint: website and crawl indicate a US presence; no Czech IČO on site (registry flag).[31, 32]

Technology profile

  • CorroboratedCore product capabilities claimed: VPS uses up to four electro‑optical/IR cameras, pre‑generated 2D/3D maps, and Nvidia‑powered AI to provide passive GPS‑free positioning.[8, 9, 10]
  • Company claimSurfaces and footprint: public presence is primarily marketing site, press and careers pages; no public developer docs or product APIs indexed in the evidence.[32, 33, 34]
  • VerifiedEngineering signals: site hosted via Webflow and Cloudflare; a GitHub organization exists (AeroboDrones) with public repos, but no linked product repos or documentation were provided.[35, 36, 37]

What could be rebuilt, and what cannot be judged from outside

  • Analyst viewReplicable with off-the-shelf parts. Multi‑camera passive localization using cameras, prebuilt maps and Nvidia inference could be reassembled from off‑the‑shelf cameras, OpenCV-like libraries, mapping tools and Nvidia SDKs (public components).[8, 9, 10]Assumes: Assumes access to comparable aerial mapping datasets and compute.
  • Analyst viewEdge supported by public evidence. Public evidence of commercial traction (>30 customers) and named defence/prime partnerships supports market adoption and integration as a defensible commercial edge.[6, 7, 4]Assumes: Assumes reported partnerships represent technical integrations, not only NDA talks.
  • Analyst viewCannot be judged from outside. Cannot externally validate algorithmic accuracy, mapping/data provenance, or robustness in contested RF environments without code, models, labeled datasets and flight logs.[20, 38, 15]Assumes: Assumes no third‑party reproducibility reports were published.

Risks and caveats

  • Analyst viewUnverified IP claims: company lists US patent numbers on its site, but an independent patent search did not find Vermeer as assignee—this inconsistency is a material diligence red flag.[12, 13, 14, 15]Assumes: Assumes the public patent search correctly matched assignee names.
  • Analyst viewConcentration / dependency risk: reported partnerships with defence primes and mention of US Air Force Techstars may create dependence on a small set of large defence customers or programmes.[7, 4]Assumes: Assumes partnerships translate to meaningful revenue share.
  • Analyst viewLimited public engineering artefacts: no product docs or API surfaces and few public repos tied to the product, complicating technical verification from open sources.[32, 33, 37]Assumes: Assumes internal repositories are private and not publicly indexed.

Questions for founders

  • Please provide definitive patent chain‑of‑title and published patent documents or assignment records to reconcile the site’s patent numbers with the independent search.[12, 15]
  • Request redacted customer purchase orders or three reference POs showing recurring revenue and scope for the reported >30 customers to confirm commercial depth.[6, 28]
  • Ask for representative flight logs, map‑build pipelines, and labeled validation datasets used for training/validation to assess accuracy and generalization claims.[9, 20]
  • Clarify the technical nature and legal status of reported defence/prime partnerships (integration contracts vs. exploratory engagements).[7, 4]
