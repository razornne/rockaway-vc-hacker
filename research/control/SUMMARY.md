## Observations (small sample, not statistics)

1. Where the company had a Czech s.r.o., the earliest money-related signal was a register entry about capital or shareholders, usually from earlier rounds. The entry for the round itself appeared 0–0.5 months before the press release, or after it.
2. Grants and public contracts are clearly visible only at DYNANIC, a university spin-off.
3. The youngest companies (Duvo, TopK, Grid.online) have nothing dated earlier than 10 months before T0. Several rounds are follow-ons by the same funds, so traces of earlier rounds exist; a first-cheque deal would not have them.

## Limits

- No Czech entity was found for TopK, Edmund and Supernova.
- CORDIS, EIC, STARFOS and TED were not checked. The first press mention was not searched systematically.
- Sources disagree on who led some rounds; some companies are included because the fund is a key investor rather than the lead.
- DYNANIC is pre-seed. Supernova matches the register by name only.
