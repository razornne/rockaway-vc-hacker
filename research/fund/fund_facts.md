# Rockaway Ventures: fund facts (items 2-6)

Collected 2026-11-16. Secondary aggregators (Tracxn, Caplight, VCBacked, PitchBook, Crunchbase) are hints only, marked (hint).

## 2. Fund facts

- Fund size: EUR 53m ("current fund size"); stage late seed and Series A (seed to Series A); geography CEE, DACH, European founders in the US; sectors AI-led transformative tech, deep tech, defence/dual-use, industrial/manufacturing AI, energy. Also: min. investment CZK 1m for qualified investors, claimed return >140% on invested capital, targets 4x / IRR 35%+. https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/rockaway-ventures
- Euro.cz interview with Petr Smid (2026-10-23): EUR 53m, over 80% invested, initial tickets EUR 0.5-2m. https://www.euro.cz/clanky/ceske-startupy-umeji-dokazat-hodne-s-malem-jen-si-nekdy-malo-veri-rika-petr-smid-z-rockaway-ventures/
- Second fund "Rockaway Ventures II", announced 2025-05-16 at nearly EUR 55m (CZK 1.4bn): late seed and Series A, aim to cover pre-seed to growth; 60% CEE, 40% Western Europe and US diaspora. Tech.eu: launched 2022. https://tech.eu/2025/05/16/rockaway-ventures-closes-55m-fund-to-back-transformative-tech-in-cee-and-beyond/ ; Czech version dated 14 May 2025 https://startupkitchen.community/rockaway-ventures-announces-second-fund-it-has-1-4-billion-crowns-ready-for-startups/
- First investments since 2014 without a formal fund (investing "since 2014"): same two articles; https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/news/petr-smid-joining-from-google-becomes-new-investment-partner-of-rockaway-ventures
- Fund-page figure EUR 53m vs EUR ~55m in press: unreconciled (possibly different dates/measures).
- Position in group: Rockaway Fund (master, target EUR 1.2bn+) has subfunds Rockaway Alpha, Rockaway Defense, United Founders, Rockaway Ventures, RockawayQ, RockawayX. https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/rockaway-fund
- LPs: Rockaway Capital about 25% of capital, remainder private investors mainly Czech; none named. https://tech.eu/2025/05/16/rockaway-ventures-closes-55m-fund-to-back-transformative-tech-in-cee-and-beyond/ ; https://startupkitchen.community/rockaway-ventures-announces-second-fund-it-has-1-4-billion-crowns-ready-for-startups/
- Portfolio count claims: fund page 15; Vermeer release (2025-10-23) says 13; tech.eu (May 2025) 11; Euro.cz (Oct 2026) 22 companies (likely incl. wider Rockaway). See portfolio_list.json (22 rows: 16 fund-era/legacy confirmed or likely, 4 legacy candidates, plus exits).
- The public fund page logo wall is stale (images uploaded Sept/Oct 2025) and omits 2025-26 deals (Remix, BottleCap AI, Apptronik, Navigara, ValkaAI, Raus, FreewayCamper).
- Reported new fund: Lupa.cz via search summary says a new Rockaway Ventures fund targets EUR 100m (about CZK 2.5bn). I could not read the primary text. Unverified.

## 4. United Founders and the Rockaway group

Yes, connected, with a stated degree of independence.
- Listed under "Our funds" on Rockaway Capital's site and in the Rockaway Fund subfund table (early-stage European startups). https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/united-founders ; https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/rockaway-fund
- People: co-founded 2025 by Jakub Havrlant (founder of Rockaway Capital), Vit Horky (founder/GP; Brand Embassy founder, itself an early Rockaway-linked company) and Vaclav Pavlecka (Managing Partner). https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/news/united-founders-invests-agent-f-continium ; https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/havrlant-spoluzaklada-united-founders-novy-fond-chce-do-evropskych-technologii-investovat-2-miliardy/ (2025-11-05)
- Capital: CC.cz (2025-11-04): committed EUR 17m = Rockaway EUR 15m + Horky EUR 1m + team EUR 1m; target at least EUR 80m. Rockaway is largest contributor. Horky says it is not "another fund under Rockaway management"; shared legal/accounting/management synergies; indirect successor to Czech Founders VC. https://cc.cz/novy-fond-jakuba-havrlanta-dal-do-nej-stovky-milionu-a-spojuje-stovku-uspesnych-podnikatelu/
- Shared deals: no deal found where both United Founders and Rockaway Ventures appear. UF deals: Wonderful (Series C 2026-09-03 posted on Rockaway site), EveryHealth, Agent F (USD 4m pre-seed, Alpha Edison lead), Continium (CZK 61m round, CZK 10m from UF), 2026-11-12. https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/news/united-founders-invests-agent-f-continium
- Positioning: pre-seed/earliest stage; Rockaway Ventures and RockawayQ "did not plan to cover" that stage but may follow on (same CC.cz piece). That makes UF a plausible upstream deal source for Rockaway Ventures.
- Czech Founders VC (UF predecessor) appears as co-investor in Cruxo's seed per VCBacked (hint): https://www.vcbacked.co/company/cruxo
