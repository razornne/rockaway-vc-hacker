# Rockaway Defense - research note (as of 2026-11-16)

Key point for the applicant: Rockaway Defense is a **private-equity / buyout-style sub-fund**, not a venture fund. Defense *venture* exposure sits in **Rockaway Ventures**.

## 1. What it is
- **Vehicle:** "Rockaway Alpha Fund - podfond Defense", sub-fund of Rockaway Alpha Fund SICAV a.s.; open-ended (evergreen) qualified-investor fund, CZK base; manager/administrator Winstor investicni spolecnost a.s., depositary CSOB. Rockaway acts as adviser (fee up to 2.5% p.a.). https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/rockaway-defense
- **Launch:** fund formed 14 May 2025 (https://www.fki-fondy.cz/fondy/rockaway-defense/); initial subscription to 30 Sep 2025; first redemptions possible May 2027 (Rockaway page). Public promotion piece 20 Nov 2025 (sponsored): https://m.echo24.cz/a/HkYQC/rockaway-capital-spousti-unikatni-fond-zamereny-na-klicove-hrace-v-oblasti-obrannych-technologii
- **Size:** target EUR 450m (Rockaway page); committed capital EUR 120m as of Nov 2025 (Echo24, sponsored); distributor shows asset value ~CZK 1.4bn at 30 Jun 2026, NAV not disclosed (FKI). Not verified independently.
- **Returns/terms:** target net IRR 11-15% p.a. (gross 15-25%); min. ticket CZK 1m; 5+ yr horizon; performance fee above HICP+4%.
- **LPs:** qualified investors incl. private wealth; no named LPs found.
- **Stage/ticket:** growth/PE, not seed. Criteria: strong market position/high entry barriers, own R&D and dual-use tech/patents, proven European defense track record, capital-light production with long contracts and strong cash flow (Rockaway page). Fund-level ticket size per deal: not found.
- **Thesis:** electronic warfare, sensors, communications (Rockaway FAQ); Echo24 adds cyber defense, drones, AI. European focus; stated aim "among the largest funds in Central Europe focused only on defense".

## 4. Relationship to Rockaway Ventures
- No evidence that deals moved from Ventures to Defense. The two are separate: Defense = PE on profitable defense-tech companies (URC came from the group before the fund existed); Ventures = early-stage.
- Ventures still lists "defence and dual-use solutions" among its sectors (alongside AI, deep tech, industrial AI, energy), late seed-Series A, Central Europe/DACH, fund ~EUR 53m. https://www.rockawaycapital.com/en/fund/rockaway-ventures
- Ventures defense/dual-use deals: Vermeer (Series A, 23 Oct 2025; described by Forbes CZ as its 13th company and 2nd dual-use) and CulturePulse (Feb 2025). https://forbes.cz/rockaway-slape-do-obrany-spolu-s-fondem-ze-sillicon-valley-hlasi-investici-do-dronu/
- Statements: Havrlant (founder/CEO) - "investment in own defense technologies is becoming a new form of stability" (Echo24); Trojan - deals in this sector close on trust and relationships (Echo24); Pavel Falout (CIO Rockaway Fund) - entering new sectors such as defense tech, e.g. drone signal jamming (https://cc.cz/investicni-fondy/reality-jsou-pro-nas-nudny-byznys-zajimavejsi-je-napriklad-defense-tech-rikaji-manazeri-fondu-rockaway/); Petr Smid (Ventures) on Vermeer: technology to become standard for drones/robots. **No explicit statement found on why a separate defense vehicle** (Smid/Havrlant on that point: not found).
