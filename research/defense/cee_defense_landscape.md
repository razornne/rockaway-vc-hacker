## B. Market context
- DAEN report (via Lupa, 26 Oct 2026): Czech startups raised no VC in defense/dual-use in the first nine months of 2026, the only one of 11 tracked countries with that result; since 2020 seven Czech startups with USD 65M VC. Czech capital goes to foreign companies (Occam cited). https://www.lupa.cz/clanky/evropa-sype-desitky-miliard-do-obrannych-startupu-do-ceska-letos-nesla-ani-koruna/
- Implication: Czech funds mostly invest abroad; local Czech defense startups (Dronetag, U&C UAS, EAGLE.ONE, Fly4Future, TRL Space, Vrgineers) show no disclosed large rounds. https://www.adriadefense.com/7-czech-defense-tech-startups-and-scaleups-to-watch-in-2026/
- Europe defense/resilience startups raised USD 8.7B in 2025 (+55%). https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/europ-s-defence-top-deals-in-early-2026
- EDF 2026: EUR 1B total, over EUR 20M for a Defence Equity Facility (via Czech business press, secondary). https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/evropsky-obranny-fond-2026-prilezitosti-pro-ceske-firmy-ve-vyzkumu-a-inovacich/
