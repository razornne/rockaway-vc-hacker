## 4. CSG link: deal flow and exits (sourced statements only)
- Website pitch: CSG market access, manufacturing capability and 30+ years of defense contacts "help overcome barriers"; industrial experts help select and support deals (https://techhorizons.eu).
- Luhovy (May 2025): CSG's contacts with firms, institutions and governments help portfolio startups win tenders; CSG experts help assess deals (e15 interview above).
- Strnad on Firehawk: both sides exploring industrial uses; potential US-Europe cooperation (e15 Firehawk link above). CSG itself invested directly in NVD in Aug 2026 and, per CSG's investment director, sees Canada as a potential customer for its ammunition and land equipment (The Logic link above). CSG COO David Chour: startup investing complements CSG R&D (e15 https://www.e15.cz/byznys/novy-fond-z-dilny-csg-a-presto-ventures-oznamil-tri-investice-vsechny-jsou-do-ai-1419518).
- Exits: no source states an exit strategy, and none says CSG will acquire portfolio companies. Presto only says it supports companies to "a successful exit" (CSG release). Not verified; treat strategic acquisition by CSG as hypothesis.
