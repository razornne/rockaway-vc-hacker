**Earliest signal:** prior_round 2020-10-05 (SEK Ventures), 20.5 months before T0 (the grant_national 2020-09 item is 21.6 months but weaker).

**Likely deal source:** Rockaway's own earlier vertical investment. Forbes CZ frames it as following "recent investments in Campiri and Spotawheel", and Campiri was led by Rockaway 5.7 weeks earlier with the same partner (D. Zabrodsky). Co-investor network: existing SEK Ventures (Flix founders). Miton (a Campiri investor) later appears at FreewayCamper too. How the introduction happened is not stated.

## Campiri / FreewayCamper relationship (sources)
- 2022-05-11: Rockaway Ventures leads the Campiri seed extension with Miton and Purple Ventures (cc.cz link above). 2022-06-20: Rockaway leads equity in FreewayCamper (Forbes link). Forbes 2024-02-19: both are backed by Czech investors Miton and Rockaway; the deal had been negotiated since summer 2023 (https://forbes.cz/konsolidace-na-trhu-s-obytnaky-ceske-campiri-se-spojuje-s-nemci/).
- 2024: merger; L. Janousek (Campiri) and N. Voitiouk (FreewayCamper) become group co-CEOs (https://shorttermrentalz.com/news/freewaycamper-investment-campiri-merger/). A Forbes interview dates the merger April 2024; Campiri became owned by the German FreewayCamper GmbH. Summer/autumn 2024: EUR 4M from Rockaway Capital, Nomiverse, SEK Ventures, Miton (same STR link).
- 2025-03: FreewayCamper preliminary insolvency, administrator a month later. Campiri itself was not insolvent but was caught in the proceedings; Nomiverse (Janousek) bought it back; investors' recovery described as practically zero (https://forbes.cz/drsny-svet-startupu-jak-ceske-campiri-proslo-ocistou-a-investori-prisli-o-penize/, 2026-02-03). The PitchBook "exit 2025-03-10" hint is unconfirmed and more likely the insolvency.
- See also research/companies/campiri.md (Czech registry: Miton / Purple Ventures vehicles removed 14 May 2024, Nomiverse sole shareholder from 16 Dec 2025).
