## Registry accessibility (Germany)
- Handelsregister (handelsregister.de) is free but a JS form with limited document downloads; capital changes and shareholders show up in documents (Gesellschafterliste, notarised resolutions), not as a Czech-style dated-entry history. northdata.de aggregates it but rate-limited my requests. Difficulty: medium. A new shareholder is typically visible only as a Gesellschafterliste filing.
- Financial statements: Unternehmensregister; small GmbHs often file abbreviated accounts with a lag. Gov contracts (TED / German portals): not relevant for this company. National grants and accelerators: not checked.
