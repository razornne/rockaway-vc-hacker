## Earliest public signal before T0
Wayback capture of gjirafa.com, 2013-01-12 (website_first, 36.9 months before; content not opened, and press says the public launch was 2014). Earliest signal with content verified: StartupYard 2014 batch profile on 2014-05-15 (accelerator, 20.8 months).

## Likely deal source
Clear and public: the StartupYard accelerator in Prague, where Rockaway is an investor. Cc.cz states Gjirafa was founded within StartupYard and that is how Rockaway found it. Angels from the accelerator period (Esther Dyson, Ondrej Bartos, Philip Staehelin) were already in. Rockaway re-invested in 2019 (USD 6.7m Series B).

## Registry accessibility
- Kosovo: ARBK is the official register; its site (arbk.rks-gov.net) is a JS app that I did not query. A third-party mirror (arbk.atlas-studio.eu, daily refresh) exists with a search; business IDs are 9-digit NUI. Capital, owners and filings availability were not tested. Only an ambiguous number was found on a Gjirafa page, not recorded as an ID.
- Albania: QKB register with mirrors (qkb.atlas-studio.eu, opencorporates.al). Not checked for Gjirafa.
- United States: Gjirafa, Inc. appears to be Delaware; Delaware gives no shareholder or capital-increase data, so registry signals are essentially unavailable there.
- Grants, procurement: not checked; low relevance for 2013-2016.
- Verdict: for Kosovo/Albania the registry route is fragmented (official SPA, mirrors) and shareholder-level signals probably thin. Accelerator and website signals were more useful than registries; for these markets a radar should lean on accelerators and press.
