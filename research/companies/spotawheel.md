## Registry accessibility
- Greece: GEMI (publicity.businessportal.gr) is the official registry and holds incorporation, capital increases (S.A. announcements) and financial statements, so Czech-style registry signals exist in principle. The portal is a JS app; I did not drive it, and opengemi.gov.gr did not connect. Not confirmed as scrapable.
- Third-party: Elevate Greece startup registry gives a self-reported profile (funding rounds, no legal ID).
- Gov-contract/grant analogs (Diavgeia, KIMDIS, national grant lists) not checked; low relevance for this business.
- Verdict: registry signals for Greece are likely available but untested; in practice the earliest signals here were website and press, not registry.
