## Registry accessibility (Bulgaria)
- Commercial Register (Registry Agency, portal.registryagency.bg/CR): public and free, search by UIC; Bulgarian-language JS interface. I could reach it (HTTP 200) but not parse it with simple fetches. Capital and shareholder signals for a joint-stock company (AD) may be only partly public (share transfers in an AD are not necessarily registered). Not verified. Difficulty: medium-high.
- Annual financial statements are filed to the register (not retrieved). Gov contracts (procurement register / TED), EU-funds grants (UMIS/ISUN) and accelerators were not checked; likely available but Bulgarian-only and hard to search by company name.
- Caveat: Remix is a 13-year-old, ~USD 60M-revenue company already carved out of a US-listed group; registry signals would show restructuring more than early discovery.
